Replace the rule-built copy with a model-written record

The panel used to compose its own sentences from rules: a tool call became
「改完了「旁白逻辑」」, a queue item became 「下一件是…」. That is why it read as
fragments — the words were ours, not a reading of the work, and two of the
three rows were predictions that were usually wrong by the time you read them.

v0.3.0 throws that away and keeps one thing: a record of what is actually
finished.

New:

- lib/steward.js — the model-facing layer, and it is pure. stewardPrompt() asks
  one narrow question ("what small things has this conversation finished?"),
  stewardInput() hands over only the recent digest plus the last few recorded
  lines so cost stays bounded, parseSteward() turns the answer into entries,
  appendSteward() appends without ever rewriting, and shouldSummarise() decides
  when to spend a call.
- The prompt forbids plans, todos and anything in flight. parseSteward() is the
  second gate: a cheap model does occasionally slip a plan into a facts list,
  and showing it would break the one promise the record makes.
- Host half calls the model every N finished steps with a minimum gap, appends
  the result, and never resets the record. /__sidekick_lens/summarise forces one
  on demand (used by the tests).
- Config: summaryProvider / summaryModel / summaryEverySteps / summaryMinGapMs.
  The cheapest-model choice is explicit because the Host exposes no price field
  to sort on — leaving them empty follows the session default.

Removed, with no equivalent:

- The three rows, the ledger, the battle log, the chronicle, the checklist
  states and the squad tree are no longer rendered. 正在做 / 下一步 / 待做 are
  gone from the dictionaries, the components and the copy.
- Six components and 126 orphan CSS blocks deleted; client.js's stylesheet went
  from 26.7 KB to 12.5 KB.

Ask box:

- Prefilled with 「当前正在做什么」. Typing replaces it wholesale — the prefill
  is never kept and never concatenated with what you type. Sending restores it.
- Still a detached llm.stream with no session id and no tools; watching costs
  nothing.

226 tests (up from 203). One real defect found while doing this: a bulk delete
took out the PULSE_SLOTS / PULSE_WINDOW_MS declarations with it, and the white
panel that produced was only found by disabling components one at a time.
